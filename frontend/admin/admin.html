    Archive settings

    What to archive

    A record is due once it is older than its keep period. Automatic archiving takes every due record; Archive now starts from these choices.

    Submissions with their pledges, answers, likes and photos, and the visitors who made them. Archived submissions also leave the pledgeboard; Leaves on the tree below sets what happens to their leaves. The visitor count and the all-time pledge totals still count them.

    Reports with their item photos. Contact details are encrypted in the archive.

    When off, photos are deleted with the records and not kept.
    After a checked archive

    What happens to a visitor's leaf on the digital tree when their feedback is archived and deleted. A change only affects later archives. A VIP's gold leaf is never archived: it stays on the tree with its name.

    • Keep the leaf, without a name. The leaf stays and shows "Visitor". Only its date, its badge and whether it was a VIP leaf are kept. No name, email, pledge or photo.
    • Keep the leaf, with initials. The same, and a visitor whose name was shown on the tree keeps their initials, such as J. T. Every other leaf shows "Visitor", and so does a name whose initials would give it away (Chinese, Japanese or Korean characters, or one-letter words).
    • Remove the leaf. The leaf leaves the tree with the records. The tree's year book still counts it as archived.

When this year's leaf count reaches a stage's minimum (for example 10 leaves for Stage 2), both trees move to that stage within about 15 seconds: on the /3dTree screen the tree grows into the new stage's tree in place, without reloading: it keeps its size, its foliage and the visitors' leaves, then grows bigger with a growth spurt of fresh foliage. The tree is fully grown at the Stage 4 (Full Canopy) number: raise it to make the tree grow more slowly, over more leaves. Manual: the tree stays at the chosen stage, shown full. The same growth stage as the 2D tree, so both trees always match. The 3D tree shows the Ketapang Stage 0 to 4 model from assets/Tree/3dTreeLeaves. Stage 0 is a bare trunk with no feedback leaves.
